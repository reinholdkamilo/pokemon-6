# Adventure Trainer Sprite Attribution

These unmodified trainer sprites were downloaded from the Pokémon Showdown trainer sprite directory:

https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/

Pokémon Showdown states that many trainer sprites have individual artists and requires appropriate artist credit. The original source filename for every imported sprite is retained in `manifest.json`. These files must not be edited unless the applicable artist grants permission.

Imported for the fan-made Pokémon 6 project. Pokémon and related characters are trademarks of their respective owners.
